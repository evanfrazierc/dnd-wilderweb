Taxes: 1 Wealth OR 2 of any one resource that you choose
Tithes: 1 Wealth (optional)

Taxes, different from Tithes, are either 1 Wealth OR 2 of any resource you choose per settlement and paid annually. This is because the tax in the form of coin is valued more, but lumps of resources are still accepted.

And don't worry about the "per settlement" part yet. You guys currently have smaller offshoot areas like the quarry or lumber camps, but those aren't settlements even if a few people live there. It's for if you guys get big, go across the lake or something, and decide to build a second castle/townhall/manor with it's own name and local leaders and such. Here is a basic summary of the region of the world we'll be in (it is all homebrew and new, none of it related to our last campaign).

It is set in a region known as the Free Kingdoms, a temperate part of a larger continent. This region also borders a sea to the north, called the Norean Sea. There are large empires to the south, a ways off the map. There is a frigid and small northern continent off the map, above the Norean Sea. Anything off map generally won't factor too much into the plot. AND, if you invent a place, I'll do what I can to put it somewhere on the map and integrate it into the world.

On the map, we will be starting in the city of Arnestal, in the county of the same name, of the Kingdom of Casdenia. You can choose to be from Arnestal, or have traveled there from one of its neighboring kingdoms (two of which are on the map, two off the map). The main focus of this campaign is on the titular expanse of territory known as the Wilderlands. There will be map exploration, so you can't see the overall map from the get-go.

NOTE: This game is loosely based off the Pathfinder: Kingmaker adventure module and CRPG. The goal is to settle a wilderness, explore its secrets, and see a realm be built -- whether you want to rule or support a ruler or simply live there as others rule, it's up to you. I've majorly simplified the governance mechanics of that module...because they are crazy complex. That being said, I fell in love with the Pathfinder world because of Kingmaker. This isn't that setting, but it was just that enjoyable, so I hope this'll be a fun way to share the experience.
