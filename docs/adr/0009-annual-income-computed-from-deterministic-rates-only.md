# Annual Income & Upkeep is computed only from deterministic per-building rates

The old "Annual Income & Upkeep" panel was a hand-written snapshot frozen at migration time, with its own note admitting it should be "treated as a base reference rather than the current total." Nothing recomputed it as buildings changed.

Making it live means giving `building_catalog` a structured `annual_effect` column (`{resourceName: signedIntDelta}` per building, separate from the existing free-text `effect`/`upkeep` columns used for the DM-facing description) and summing `rate × count` across currently-built buildings on every read (`server/db/annualIncome.js`).

Auditing the full building catalog against the old hand-written breakdown's actual numbers found that most of it reconciles exactly with a literal flat-rate reading of each building's effect text -- but three things the old manual math used aren't representable as a per-building rate at all: a Mill's effect is a formula over the count of Farm buildings (not its own flat rate), Population's food consumption has no corresponding building record, and Barracks' catalog upkeep only lists Wealth even though the old breakdown also charged Food per Guard unit. Buildings whose effect is dice-gated ("1-on-1d4 generates...") or a player-invoked action (a Market's trade) are excluded for the same reason: there's no way to turn either into a guaranteed annual number without inventing rules this app doesn't know.

Rather than approximate any of this -- expected value for dice, a guessed population-upkeep formula -- the computed total simply excludes anything that isn't a flat, unconditional, per-building rate, and the panel says so explicitly. This is a permanent scope boundary, not a temporary gap: getting kingdom economy math wrong and having the DM trust it silently is worse than an honestly incomplete number. The catalog's `annual_effect` field stays freely editable per building if the DM later wants to include something (e.g. approximating a dice effect with a flat average), but the app itself will not guess on their behalf.
